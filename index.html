Our story

Two friends.
One big idea.

Bambas S&R started with something simple: two school friends who wanted to share the knowledge they spent years building.

Stephan grew up in Germany and brings a lifetime of natural German communication. Robert developed fluent, C2-level English through 11 years of experience. Together, we turn serious language knowledge into lessons that feel human, practical and enjoyable.

We are still students ourselves. That means we understand learning—the challenges, the breakthroughs and the courage it takes to speak. We are not here to teach from a distance. We are here to learn, grow and communicate alongside you.

Passionate about education. Driven by language.
